Your 7-day plan

Each day takes about 20 minutes. Tick off each day as you finish. Your ticks are saved on this device only. Short on time? Do Days 1 and 7, then the topics you find hardest.

Year 9 and Year 10: your battle is first, so start now. If you have fewer than 7 days left, do two plan days in one sitting. Year 7 and Year 8: you have more time, so start the plan 7 days before 18 November.

    What your year will face

    Every year gets questions on the same 8 topics, but the mix of easy, medium and hard questions changes. Pick your year to see what to focus on.

    Easy: 1 shot, +10 points Medium: 2 shots, +20 points Hard: 3 shots, +30 points

    How the game works

    Your crew shares one fleet on a big ocean with the other crews in your year. Earn shots by answering questions, hunt down the other fleets, and try to be the last crew afloat.

    1. Join

      Scan your year's QR code on the hall screen, or type its 4-letter code. Enter your names and pick an avatar.

    2. Choose your crew

      Tap the crew your teacher gave you. If you pick the wrong one, press Leave this crew before the battle starts.

    3. Hide your fleet

      Place your ships in your bright home waters, or press Random. Then press Lock in fleet.

    4. Earn shots

      Answer questions in the Earn shots tab. A crew shares its shots and can hold up to 8. A wrong answer means a 10-second wait.

    5. Attack

      Tap a square in another crew's waters and press Fire. Squares are named column then row, so C7 is column C, row 7.

    6. Survive

      The storm closes in from the edges and exposes ships to every crew. The round ends when one crew is left or the clock runs out.

    Your three weapons

    Shot

    Uses 1 shot. It can hit or miss.

    Torpedo

    Uses no shots. Type the right answer and it never misses. Get it wrong and the torpedo is lost.

    Power-up

    Answer 3 in a row to earn one. A sonar scans a 3 × 3 area, and an airstrike hits a + shape of 5 squares.

    In the competition, each whole crew is normally limited to one shot every 15 seconds, one torpedo every 60 seconds and one power-up every 120 seconds. You can keep answering while you wait, because answers still score points. Each round normally lasts 8 minutes.

    How you score

    Answers

    • Easy +10
    • Medium +20
    • Hard +30
    • 3 in a row +25

    Combat

    • Hit +25
    • Sink a ship +50 to +125
    • Wipe out a fleet +200

    Round placing

    • 1st +300
    • 2nd +200
    • 3rd +100
    • 4th and below +50

    Points from all 3 rounds add up, and the crew with the highest total is your year's champion. In the Grand Final, the four champions start again on zero and play one deciding round.

    Practice hunt

    Two enemy ships are hiding: one 3 squares long and one 2 squares long. Sink both in as few shots as you can. Ships are at least 2 squares long, so firing at every other square will always find them.

    Tap any square to fire.

    Shots fired: 0

    Tip: after a hit, try the squares above, below, left and right to find which way the ship lies.

    Revise General ICT

    Open a topic, learn the notes from Start here upwards, then try the practice questions. These aren't the competition's questions, so learn the ideas rather than the answers. Typed questions work like torpedoes: type a short answer. Capital letters don't matter.

    Work as a crew

    You'll probably share one device with your group. Give everyone a job so that nobody just watches.

    The reader

    Reads each question aloud and leads the discussion. Remember that a wrong answer costs 10 seconds.

    The spotter

    Watches the chart and battle log, and chooses the next target.

    The driver

    Holds the device and taps. Swap jobs between rounds so that everyone gets a turn.

    Battle tips

    • Spread your ships. Mix across and down, and don't line them all up.
    • Stay away from the edge. The storm starts at the outside, so ships nearer the middle of the ocean stay hidden longer.
    • Spend your shots. At 8 shots you can't earn more, so a full crew should fire.
    • Keep answering while reloading. Your crew can only fire every so often, but answers still score.
    • Use a sonar before an airstrike. Aim the airstrike at the orange diamonds that your sonar finds.
    • Talk across your crew. Shots are shared with every group in it, so agree on targets and who fires next.
    • Read the battle log. A crew that's losing ships fast may be easy to finish off.

    On the day

    • Charge your device, and bring your charger if your teacher allows it.
    • Know your group and which crew your teacher has given you.
    • Connect to the Wi-Fi your teacher tells you to use.
    • Use one device per group and close your other tabs. The game has a connection limit, so every open tab counts.
    • Use your own year's code from the hall screen.
    • If your page refreshes or your device sleeps, open the link again. The game remembers you.
